## 4. How the Credit System Works

Sally IP utilizes a transparent, predictable usage-based credit model:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   Sally IP Credit System                    │
├──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│ Action                       │ Credit Cost                  │
├──────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┤
│ Conversational Prompt / Query│ 0.1 Credit (10 queries = 1)  │
│ Draft Document Generated     │ 1.0 Credit                   │
│ Monthly Renewal              │ Automatically resets monthly │
│ Additional Credits           │ Available on-demand anytime  │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```

* **No Lock-in**: Users can purchase on-demand add-on credit bundles at any time without upgrading their monthly tier.
* **Billing Cycle**: Unused monthly plan credits reset at the start of each billing cycle; purchased add-on packs roll over.

## 8. Enterprise Security, Privacy & Ethics

* **Zero Data Retention for Training**: Sally IP does **not** train its foundational or domain models on client prompts, documents, or uploads.
* **Bank-Grade Encryption**: End-to-end encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Granular workspace permissions for firms and corporate legal departments.
* **Audit Logs & Export Compliance**: Detailed telemetry logs of all generated drafts for legal auditing and billing records.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: How does credit deduction work?
* Each prompt or conversational query consumes **0.1 credit** (meaning 1 credit gives you 10 prompts).
* Generating a full structured document or legal draft consumes **1 credit**.

#### Q: What happens if I exceed my monthly credits?
* You can purchase on-demand add-on credit bundles directly from your billing dashboard without needing to upgrade your plan tier.

#### Q: Can multiple team members share a subscription?
* The **Business**, **Enterprise**, and **Enterprise+** plans support multi-user team workspaces with shared credit pools and unified billing.

#### Q: Is client confidentiality maintained?
* Yes. Sally IP operates under strict enterprise confidentiality protocols. All client data is encrypted and completely isolated. Your inputs and outputs are never shared, exposed, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

#### Q: What document export formats are supported?
* Drafts can be exported as Microsoft Word (`.docx`), searchable PDF (`.pdf`), plain text (`.txt`), or Markdown (`.md`).
