SallyIP — IP AI · Patents · Trademarks · Benchmarks · Resources · Glossary

IP intelligence, from creation to enforcement.

SallyIP is a verification-first AI workspace for intellectual property professionals working across patents, trademarks and the wider IP lifecycle.

Available product capabilities

Patent prior-art search, novelty and inventive-step analysis, freedom-to-operate matrices, US patent drafting with §101 screening and §112 support checks, office-action response with claim-QA audit, claim charts, patent family graphs, prosecution-history timelines, trademark clearance and intelligence, litigation evidence chronologies, contract review and reusable legal playbooks.

Guidance and developing capabilities

Copyright, design-rights and trade-secret questions are covered as guidance and research support with educational resources — not dedicated production workflows. Trademark monitoring and portfolio intelligence are developing capabilities. Practitioner review is required; nothing here is filing-ready output.

How verification works

Evidence first: hybrid retrieval, exact-quote verification, citation-integrity guard, entailment grading, and VERIFIED / QUALIFIED / RESEARCH REQUIRED answer modes. No evidence, no assertion. Measured results — including failures — are published at SallyIP benchmarks.
