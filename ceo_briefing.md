## 2. The Problem in Simple Words: Why Does Sally Need to Exist?

### A. Getting a Patent is Painfully Slow and Expensive
When an engineer or company invents something new (like a new chip, software, medical device, or battery), they have to file a patent to stop competitors from copying it.
- **Cost:** A single patent costs **$10,000 to $25,000** just in lawyer fees.
- **Time:** It takes an attorney **20 to 40 hours** of manual, tedious typing and cross-checking.
- **Backlog:** Patent offices take over **2 years** to approve applications because there is a mountain of paperwork.

### B. Why Lawyers Can't Just Use ChatGPT or Normal AI
Lawyers tried using ChatGPT, and it was a disaster. 
- ChatGPT has a known habit of **"hallucinating"**—it sounds completely confident, but makes up fake court cases, fake patent numbers, and fake laws.
- In patent law, if a lawyer files a document with even one fake quote or wrong claim, **the patent is dead**, the company loses millions, and the lawyer can be sued for malpractice or lose their license.
- **Normal AI is too risky for serious legal work.**

### 1. The Patent Detective (Prior Art Search)
- **What it does:** Before you spend $20k filing a patent, you have to know: *"Did someone else already invent this?"*
- **How Sally helps:** In 2 seconds, Sally scans over 100 million patents from the US, Europe, and worldwide databases, plus millions of scientific papers (arXiv, IEEE). It gives you a clear green/yellow/red score showing whether your idea is truly new.

### 2. The Patent Drafter (Drafting Studio)
- **What it does:** Writing a patent is complicated. It requires strict legal language, complex "claim trees", and numbered diagrams.
- **How Sally helps:** The lawyer inputs the core idea, and Sally generates the first draft of the complete patent specification and legal claims in minutes. What normally takes 20 hours takes under 2 hours for the lawyer to review and polish.

### 3. The Rejection Solver (Office Action Assistant)
- **What it does:** 80% of the time, the government patent examiner pushes back and says: *"No, your invention is too similar to Patent X."*
- **How Sally helps:** Sally reads the government rejection letter, breaks down the examiner's argument, checks what the examiner usually likes, and drafts a point-by-point counter-argument showing why your invention is different.

### 4. The Brand & Trademark Radar
- **What it does:** Before launching a new brand, product, or company name, you must ensure nobody else owns it.
- **How Sally helps:** Checks trademark registries across the US, Europe, and globally. It checks sounds-alike names (like "Kool" vs "Cool"), looks-alike logos, and meaning similarities.

### 5. The Competitor Copycat Detector (Freedom to Operate & Claims Charts)
- **What it does:** Answers two big questions:
  1. *"Are we safe to launch this product without being sued?"*
  2. *"Is our competitor infringing our patent?"*
- **How Sally helps:** Compares product features side-by-side against patent claims in a courtroom-ready chart.

### 6. The Friendly Inventor Interviewer
- **What it does:** Engineers and inventors hate talking in legal jargon. They just know how their widget works.
- **How Sally helps:** Sally acts like an interviewer. It asks 4 to 6 simple questions: *"What does it do? What problem does it fix? How does it work?"* Then Sally automatically translates that everyday English into formal legal patent language.

## 4. The "Secret Sauce": Why Sally Never Lies

Other AI tools try to "sound smart." Sally is built to **prove everything**.

```
How Other AI Works:
User Query ──▶ ChatGPT guesses from memory ──▶ Sounds confident, but often fake!

How Sally Works:
User Query ──▶ Searches Real Legal Databases ──▶ Generates Draft ──▶ Truth-Checker verifies every word
                                                                           │
               ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴──────────┐
               ▼                                                                      ▼
     [Quote Matches 100% Verbatim]                                          [Evidence is Missing]
        ✅ Show answer with proof badge                                  ⛔ "I don't have proof. I won't guess."
```

1. **Exact-Quote Verification:** If Sally quotes a legal rule, it verifies that the source text matches 100% word-for-word. If even one word is changed, it strips the quotes.
2. **No Fake Sources:** In normal AI, you often see fake footnotes. In Sally, every footnote `[S1]`, `[S2]` must link to a real document in the database. If not, it is deleted automatically.
3. **The "Fail-Closed" Safety Rule:** If Sally searches the law and doesn't find proof, **it refuses to guess.** It frankly says: *"I could not find verified evidence for this."* Lawyers love this because an honest "I don't know" is 1,000 times better than a confident lie.
4. **Total Privacy (Zero-Retention):** Customer trade secrets never leave their private workspace. Client files are never shared, never leaked, and never used to train public models.
