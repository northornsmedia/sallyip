SallyIP vs Harvey vs CoCounsel vs Genie AI (2026)

Short version: Harvey and CoCounsel lead enterprise legal AI adoption. Genie AI leads accessible contract drafting. SallyIP is built for a narrower job — verifiable patent work — and publishes its measurement methodology instead of a score.

What peer-reviewed research actually says about hallucinations

  • General LLMs (GPT-3.5/4, Llama 2, PaLM 2): 58–88% hallucination on legal queries (Dahl et al., Stanford 2024).
  • Purpose-built legal RAG tools: 17–33% — Lexis+ AI ~17% (65% accurate), Westlaw AI-Assisted Research ~33% (42% accurate), CoCounsel/Practical Law ~17% with 60%+ refusals (Magesh et al., Stanford/JELS 2024–2025).
  • Harvey, Genie AI, Solve Intelligence, DeepIP: no public hallucination measurements. Any ranked percentages you see for them come from opinion pieces, not experiments.
